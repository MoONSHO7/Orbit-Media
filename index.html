Addon integration

Orbit-Media registers icon glows with LibOrbitGlow, and status-bar textures and borders with LibSharedMedia. Orbit is not required.

Tracer and Pin Neon dispel glows are included with LibOrbitGlow. Consuming addons supply their own contours and masks.
